## What it looks like

An opening of big type on black, one word per beat in the drop, real app screens plus free stock footage, self-composed music, ending on a brand line and logo.

## Prompt to reproduce

Two steps: a first ask that sets the tone with references, then the main ask that merges the later feedback.

```
Make an app promo video that even [famous creator] would be impressed by, fast-paced like an iPhone ad teaser.
```

```
Make a promo video for our app [app name/URL].
- Not a feature list: tell a brand story about the core value the app gives
- The first second matters most: open with one scene everyone has lived through
- Opening: big type on a black screen, hitting on every beat
- Fast tempo. In the drop, one word per beat, with the word and background footage matched
- Only real app screens (open the app and capture them); find free footage for anything missing
- Make the background music yourself, tasteful, not cheesy
- End on one brand line + logo
- Show me the outline before you build it
```

## Refining notes

Watch the result and correct it in a few words: "cheesy", "weak hook", "faster", "the music is too sweet".

## Why it works

- **Two references** (a person and an ad genre) set the tone at once.
- It decides what to say (value), how to open (a one-second hook) and how much per beat (one word).
- **"Real screens only"** blocks fake UI.
- **Outline first** makes direction changes cheap.
