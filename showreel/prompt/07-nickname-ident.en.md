## What it looks like

A bouncy personal-branding video where only the nickname and a yellow flower icon were given; the model filled in the rest of the copy, music and motion.

## Prompt to reproduce

The author specified only the feel, the nickname, the flower icon and 15 seconds. With the same elements:

```
Make a 15-second motion-graphics video introducing my nickname "[nickname]".
- Include a [yellow flower] icon as the symbol
- Mood: [e.g. upbeat, bouncy motion], colours: [e.g. yellow + black]
- Make the music yourself and cut the edit tightly to the beat
- Fill in any other copy yourself so it fits
```

## What to change

- Nickname, symbol icon, mood and colours. The author's advice: the more precisely you describe mood, rhythm and colour, the better.

## Why it works

- It pins **just two things** (name, symbol) and delegates the rest, making use of the model's editing sense.
- A plain "do it" already gives a satisfying result that needs only light polishing.
