| Output | Motion graphics introducing a person, event or class, built from a reference URL and attached material |

## What it looks like

Give it material about what you're promoting (a person, class, shop or event) and it introduces it in the order hook line → key message → proof → call to action.
Pick the ratio and length for the platform (Reels, YouTube, an event screen).

## Prompt to reproduce

The author's shared Korean template, translated:

```
Please make a [30]-second motion-graphics video introducing [what you're promoting].

[1. Understand the material]
- First read the material below and grasp the key points.
· [reference URL]
· [attached material: images, logo, description, reviews, etc.]
- Attached material takes priority over information on the web.
- Don't invent anything unconfirmed; tell me first what material is missing.
- Show me a short outline of the copy for each scene before you build it.

[2. Purpose and audience]
- Who will watch this video: [target viewers]
- What I want them to do afterwards: [buy / sign up / join / follow, etc.]
- Where it will be posted: [Instagram Reels / YouTube / event screen, etc.]

[3. Structure]
- One sentence that grabs attention within the first 2 seconds
- The key message and how it differs from alternatives
- Credible proof (the maker's background, numbers, recommendations or reviews)
- Final scene: name, logo or key image, and a call to action (date, link, how to sign up, etc.)

[4. Presentation rules]
- Use quotes and confirmed copy exactly as written
- Take colours and mood from [brand colours / key image]
- One message per screen, no overlapping text
- Captions large enough to read on a phone
- Tone: [bright and upbeat / calm and serious / premium, etc.]

[5. Sound]
- Background music and sound effects timed to scene changes and emphasis
- Only copyright-free audio

[6. Deliverable]
- [1080×1920 (vertical) / 1920×1080 (horizontal) / 1080×1080 (square)], 30fps, [15]-second MP4
- Before delivering, capture key scenes and check for overlapping or cropped text, typos and factual errors yourself
```

## What to change

- Every bracketed slot: subject, length, reference URL, attachments, audience, desired action, platform, brand colours, tone and ratio.
- The more material you attach, the more accurate the result. Always include the logo and a key image.

## Why it works

- **Material first, nothing invented**: attachments outrank the web, and asking about missing material up front prevents factual errors.
- **Copy first**: showing per-scene copy before building makes direction changes cheap (the same as outline-first in [03](03-app-promo.en.md)).
- **Purpose and action stated**: who watches and what they should do decides the structure (hook → difference → proof → call to action).
- **Mobile readability**: one message per screen, captions readable on a phone.
- **Self-check before delivery**: captures are checked for overlap, cropping, typos and factual errors.
