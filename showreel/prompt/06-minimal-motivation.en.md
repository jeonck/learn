| Output | 15-second vertical motivational motion-graphics short with English copy and music |

## What it looks like

White lines, circles, squares and dotted lines on black keep changing shape to match the copy, with mint appearing only as an accent.

## Prompt to reproduce

```
Using only white lines and simple shapes (circles, squares, dotted lines) on a black background,
make an impactful 15-second [topic: motivational] motion-graphics video in vertical short-form ratio.
Use one accent colour only: [mint]. Include English copy and music.
```

## What to change

- Topic, accent colour and copy language. Even in Korean, shape combinations alone give an ad-like tone.

## Why it works

- Colour (mono plus one) and form (basic shapes) are tightly bound, so it looks polished on any model.
- The model splits the rhythm itself so the shapes change with the copy.
